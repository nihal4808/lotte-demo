Corporate Social Responsibility

Championing sustainable community growth, health, modern STEM schooling, and inclusive societal empowerment.

Our Social Commitment

Lotte India is a socially responsible corporate citizen. We promote sustainable growth for the community and society.

At Lotte India Corporation Private Limited, we believe that true corporate achievement is inseparable from the well-being of the communities in which we live and operate. We are dedicated to creating meaningful, enduring positive change through purposeful interventions in school education, child nutrition, rural infrastructure, preventive health, environmental preservation, and inclusive development.

Areas of Intervention

Our CSR Activities

A holistic portfolio of 8 strategic pillars designed to empower communities, protect the environment, and unlock human potential.

1. Education 🎓

Transforming government and rural schools with smart digital equipment, library resources, and interactive pedagogical tools to ensure quality learning for every child.

2. Health 🏥

Organizing routine mobile health clinics, vision screening camps, and preventive healthcare diagnostics in underserved villages adjacent to our factory locations.

3. Nutrition 🥗

Combating childhood malnutrition through dietary awareness programs, micro-nutrient education, and establishing organic nutrition gardens in schools.

4. Rural Development 🌾

Supporting sustainable rural livelihood, rural access roads, community water tanks, solar street illuminations, and public sanitation infrastructure.

5. Environmental Development 🌱

Promoting green cover through tree planting drives, rainwater harvesting units, zero-liquid discharge, energy efficiency, and solid waste segregation.

6. Skill Enhancement 🎯

Equipping young learners with 21st-century problem solving capabilities, experiential STEM laboratories, coding basics, and extracurricular arts.

7. Women's Development 👩

Supporting women self-help groups (SHGs) through vocational tailoring, sanitary hygiene programs, financial literacy, and entrepreneurial micro-grants.

8. Differently-Abled Support ♿

Distributing prosthetic aids, wheelchairs, and specialized assistive technology, alongside building barrier-free ramps in community spaces.

Flagship CSR Initiative

LOTTE SmartLearn Schools Initiative

A transformative school development initiative designed to bridge the educational, health, and nutritional divide across rural and semi-urban schools in India.

Future-Ready STEM Learning Environments

Bridging the digital divide by introducing modern science apparatus, interactive digital boards, robotics starter kits, and hands-on exploratory experiments that stimulate scientific curiosity and logical thinking.

Safe & Hygienic Environments through WASH

Constructing and renovating separate, dignified sanitation units for boys and girls, safe multi-stage RO drinking water stations, handwashing points, and ongoing hygiene behavior education to boost student attendance.

Nutrition Education & Healthy Food Choices

Cultivating school nutrition gardens tended by students and teachers, imparting vital awareness on balanced diets, seasonal greens, and essential micronutrients to eradicate child stunting and lethargy.
