The Iconic Experience

Cream Sandwiched Biscuits Drenched in Chocolate

Lotte Choco Pie is not just a snack; it is an emotion shared across millions of homes. When Lotte India introduced the 'Pie' category in 2004, it redefined sweet snacking by introducing India to the delightful pairing of soft-baked sponge and pillowy marshmallow.

Engineered with an eggless, 100% vegetarian marshmallow recipe specially crafted for Indian consumers, Lotte Choco Pie is the snack of choice for school tiffins, evening treats, office breaks, and festive celebrations.

Chef's Warm Secret

Pop your Lotte Choco Pie in the microwave for just 8 to 10 seconds. Watch the marshmallow puff into molten cream and the chocolate layer melt into pure warm decadence!
