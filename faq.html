Product Questions

Where should I go in case of a product/quality complaint? +

Contact Customer Care using the contact number and email address mentioned on the Lotte India product packaging. Alternatively, use the Contact Us page to provide product feedback and other details.

What is Lotte Choco Pie? +

Fluffy marshmallow-filled soft biscuits dipped in rich chocolate. Lotte Choco Pie is India's No.1 Choco Pie — a perfect circle of joy. Each Choco Pie features a soft biscuit base, a fluffy marshmallow filling, and a generous coating of rich chocolate.

Is Lotte Choco Pie vegetarian? +

Yes. Lotte Choco Pie is vegetarian. The product carries the green vegetarian symbol on its packaging as required by Indian food labelling regulations.

How can I identify vegetarian and non-vegetarian products? +

Food products containing ingredients derived from animal sources are identified by a red or brown circle within a square on the packaging. Vegetarian products carry a green circle within a square symbol. This is mandated by the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI).

What is the meaning of the FSSAI logo? +

The FSSAI logo indicates that the manufacturer is licensed by the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India. FSSAI is an autonomous body established under the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Government of India. It is responsible for protecting and promoting public health through the regulation and supervision of food safety.

Why is MRP missing from mono wrappers? +

As per the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011, packages weighing or containing less than 10 grams or 10 millilitres are exempt from the mandatory MRP declaration requirement on individual wrappers. Such small packages are typically sold in multi-packs where the MRP is clearly displayed on the outer packaging.

Where are Lotte India's products manufactured? +

Lotte India has three principal manufacturing facilities in India:

  • Chennai, Tamil Nadu — Choco Pie manufacturing facility (No. 26, Poonamallee-Thiruvallur Road)
  • Rohtak, Haryana — Choco Pie manufacturing facility (4-7, Sector 30A, Rohtak IMT)
  • Nellikuppam, Cuddalore — Confectionery manufacturing facility

The company also outsources confectionery manufacturing to three other units across India.

Who is the parent company of Lotte India? +

Lotte India's parent company is Lotte Wellfood, South Korea. Lotte Wellfood is part of the Korean conglomerate, Lotte Group. Lotte India came into existence post the takeover of Parrys Confectionery from Murugappa Group in 2004.
