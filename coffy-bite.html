Heritage & Evolution

More Than Three Decades of Legendary History

First introduced in the 1980s under the legendary Parrys Confectionery banner, Coffy Bite quickly became a household name across India. It was one of the earliest Indian candy brands to launch memorable, witty television commercials centered around the timeless "Coffee vs Toffee" banter.

Following Lotte's takeover of Parrys in 2004, the brand preserved its original closely-guarded recipe while modernizing production lines. In 2019, Coffy Bite underwent a vibrant national relaunch with fresh contemporary packaging, renewed retail distribution, and viral social campaigns that reconnected the brand with millions of millennial and Gen-Z consumers.
