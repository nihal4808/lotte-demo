Sweet Heritage Since 1982

Fondly Remembered as "The Child's First Toffee"

Ask any Indian adult about their earliest childhood confectionery memory, and Caramilk will inevitably surface. Its distinctive golden wrapper, comforting dairy fragrance, and gentle chew made it the very first toffee parents and grandparents willingly offered to young ones.

Over the last 40+ years, Caramilk transcended everyday snacking to become an indispensable companion in all auspicious occasions. Whether placed alongside wedding invitation trays, shared with cousins during Diwali, or handed out by proud parents after newborn celebrations, Caramilk is synonymous with blessings and sweet beginnings.
