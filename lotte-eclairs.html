The Anatomy of an Eclair

A Tasteful Experience That Blows You Away

Lotte Eclairs represents decades of confectionery refinement. Unlike ordinary candies that provide a one-dimensional sweetness, Lotte Eclairs creates an evolving tasting journey that shifts dynamically from smooth dairy toffee to rich, molten cocoa.

Manufactured in temperature-controlled environments with high-precision center-fill technology, each candy retains its luscious liquid heart in all seasons. It's the go-to sweet treat for students celebrating exam triumphs, colleagues taking a tea-break breather, and families sharing sweet joy.
